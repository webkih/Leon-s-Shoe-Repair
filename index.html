About Leon's Shoe Repair

Family-owned craftsmanship for shoes and leather goods

For years, neighbors in Mount Pleasant have trusted Leon's Shoe Repair for careful restoration work and honest recommendations. We repair shoes, boots, belts, bags, and leather accessories with attention to detail.

From sole replacements to custom fit adjustments, every repair is handled with craftsmanship built through generations of hands-on experience.
